Select filters and then click **Submit** to find supported combinations of GPUs, VBIOS versions, CUDA drivers, and confidential computing modes.
The matrix also lists form factors, RIM status, VBIOS RIMs, and SKUs.
For information about `CERT_HOLD` RIM status, refer to the [GPU verifier documentation](https://github.com/NVIDIA/nvtrust/blob/main/guest_tools/gpu_verifiers/local_gpu_verifier/README.md).

## Firmware Notes

- For Protected PCIe mode, use HGX Hopper firmware 1.7.0. Firmware 1.6.0 has a known issue that can cause the GPU to fall off the bus during boot.
- When using Secure AI with HGX Hopper firmware 1.8.0 or later, rollback to firmware 1.7.1 or earlier is not supported.
